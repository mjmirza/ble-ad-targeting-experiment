Step · the pipeline, per platform

How the data feeds each ad platform

Pick a platform. The funnel shape is the same everywhere — only the tools and match rates change. Tap a stage for detail, or press Run.

Consent is not the blocker — you have it. The blocker is stage 2 on all four: the identifier TYPE. A Bluetooth box captures a rotating BLE address, and no platform matches on that. They need an IDFA/GAID (from an on-device SDK) or a hashed email/phone. The box data is gold for on-site footfall research — just not, by itself, an ad-platform audience.
